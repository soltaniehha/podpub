VP ≠ VNP Over Every Field (November 2026) + Circuit Lower Bounds for Hamiltonian Cycle and the Permanent (November 2026)

In this episode we unpack two claimed proofs of the same 47-year-old conjecture, posted a day apart by two AI labs. On 23 November 2026 Anthropic put a 70 page paper credited to Claude on GitHub with a Lean 4 formalization, saying an internal research model discovered the result and no human mathematician has checked it. On 24 November OpenAI posted an 80 page paper of its own, also with Lean code. Both claim to settle Valiant's conjecture that VP ≠ VNP: the permanent and the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial cannot be computed by polynomial-size arithmetic circuits. This is the algebraic cousin of P versus NP, and both papers stress that it does not settle P versus NP itself.

We walk through Anthropic's bound of m^(c (log m)^(1/3)) gates over every field and its proof, which turns tensor ranks into distances using Strassen's asymptotic spectrum and then splits into a flat case and a contracting case. We compare it with OpenAI's larger exp(c (log N)^2/(log log N)^2) bound, whose proof follows a strikingly similar two-case plan even though neither paper cites the other, and we note that OpenAI's Lean development formalizes only the plain superpolynomial version. We use Peter Bürgisser's survey of Valiant's completeness classes for the background, and Limaye, Srinivasan and Tavenas's constant-depth lower bounds as the last big human breakthrough. We close on Thomas Hales's warning that a Lean proof still needs a human to check that its statement says what we mean, and on what it means when a machine-checked proof arrives before anyone has read it.
